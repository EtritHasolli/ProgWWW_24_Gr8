Cornell Notes

A note-taking system that involves dividing your notes into cues, notes, and summary sections.

Focused Notes

A method where you take highly focused notes on key concepts to reinforce understanding.

Flash Cards

A method of reviewing information by writing questions on one side and answers on the other.

Blurting

Writing everything you know about a topic to identify knowledge gaps and improve recall.

Spaced Repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals to boost long-term memory retention.

Feynman Technique

Simplifying complex concepts by explaining them in simple language.

Power Nap

A short 10-20 minute nap that boosts energy and cognitive function.

Second Brain

Organizing information externally to free up mental space and improve retention.

Time Blocking

Dividing your day into blocks of focused study time and rest.

Socratic Method

Asking and answering questions to stimulate critical thinking and understanding.

Active Recall

Testing your knowledge by recalling information without looking at your notes.

SQ3R Method

A reading comprehension method involving Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review.
